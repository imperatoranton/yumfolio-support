how do I move my book to a new iPhone?

on your old device, open Yumfolio settings and wait for “saved in iCloud”. on your new device, sign in to the same Apple Account, enable iCloud and open Yumfolio with an internet connection. recipes, photos, folders and shopping items that were saved to iCloud will be restored.

why are my changes still waiting to sync?

check your internet connection, iCloud sign-in and available iCloud storage. keep Yumfolio open and tap “sync now” in settings. until uploading finishes, new changes are stored only on this device.

can I use Yumfolio offline?

yes. recipes and shopping items are saved on your iPhone. syncing resumes when you have an internet connection and the app is open.

what happens if I delete the app?

the local copy is removed from your device. data already saved to iCloud stays there. deleting an individual recipe inside Yumfolio syncs that deletion across your devices.

how do I change the language?

tap the gear button and choose русский, беларуская or English.
